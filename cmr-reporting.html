About
CDPH 110c

The Confidential Morbidity Report (CMR), CDPH 110c, is the California Department of Public Health form used by clinicians to report a reportable communicable disease or condition to the local health department of the patient's county of residence. Complete the form and fax it to the patient's county using the numbers below. Title 17 CCR sets reporting timeframes (immediate / one working day / seven days) depending on the condition.

⚠️ The CMR contains protected health information (PHI). Confirm the destination fax number before sending, use a cover sheet, and follow your facility's confidential-fax policy.
