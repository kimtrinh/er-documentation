📊 HEART Score Interpretation

6-week MACE risk (death, MI, revascularization):

0–3 — Low Risk
MACE ~1.7%. Eligible for early discharge with outpatient follow-up if serial troponins negative. Shared decision-making discussion documented.


4–6 — Moderate Risk
MACE ~12–17%. Consider observation, stress testing, or cardiology consult. Not safe for routine discharge without further evaluation.


7–10 — High Risk
MACE ~50–65%. Early invasive strategy indicated. Cardiology consult, admission, likely catheterization.


⚠️ HEART score does not replace clinical judgment. Patients with STEMI, new LBBB, or hemodynamic instability require immediate intervention regardless of score.

💧 Creatinine Clearance — Cockcroft-Gault

Use this — not the lab's eGFR — whenever you are checking a drug label's renal threshold. Anticoagulant labels are written in Cockcroft-Gault CrCl (mL/min); eGFR is normalised to 1.73 m² body surface area and is not the same number.

Male×1.0
Female×0.85
Centimetres
Inches

Height is only used for ideal and adjusted body weight. Without it you still get the actual-body-weight result.

—
Enter sex, age, creatinine and weight
Cockcroft-Gault: (140 − age) × weight ÷ (72 × serum creatinine), × 0.85 if female.
📊 Which weight, and what it changes

There is no national consensus on which body weight to use. This tool follows the common BMI-based convention and shows you every basis so you can see when the choice crosses a dose threshold:

BMI < 18.5 — actual body weight

BMI 18.5–29.9 — actual and ideal both shown
No consensus in this range; if the two land either side of a threshold, ask pharmacy.

BMI ≥ 30 — adjusted body weight
IBW + 0.4 × (actual − IBW). Actual body weight overestimates and IBW underestimates measured clearance in obesity.


Ideal body weight (Devine): 50 kg (male) / 45.5 kg (female) + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft.


Renal thresholds that change ED management

Reference only — confirm every dose against the current label and your pharmacist.

· Enoxaparin — treatment dosing drops to 1 mg/kg daily at CrCl < 30, per the outpatient DVT pathway.
· Rivaroxaban (AF) — 15 mg daily at CrCl 15–50; avoid below 15.
· Dabigatran (AF) — US label 75 mg BID at CrCl 15–30; some guidance avoids it below 30 entirely.
· Apixaban (AF) — not a CrCl rule. Reduce to 2.5 mg BID only if 2 of 3: age ≥ 80, weight ≤ 60 kg, serum creatinine ≥ 1.5 mg/dL.


⚠️ eGFR is not interchangeable with CrCl. Substituting the lab's eGFR for Cockcroft-Gault CrCl misdoses DOACs — and the error skews toward overdosing, concentrated in patients over 75 and under 50 kg, the ones least able to tolerate it. One cohort found the two equations disagreed on dose in 45% of patients. When eGFR and CrCl fall either side of a threshold, use the equation the label specifies.

Cockcroft-Gault estimates clearance; it is less accurate at extremes of age, weight and renal function, and assumes a stable creatinine — it is not valid in acute kidney injury. KDIGO now prefers eGFR for CKD staging, but drug labels still carry Cockcroft-Gault thresholds, which is why this tool exists.

Sources: Cockcroft & Gault, Nephron 1976; Devine 1974 (ideal body weight); DOAC thresholds per current US product labeling.

🦴 Canadian C-Spine Rule — 3 steps, in order

Step 0 — applicability. Check any that apply; if any is checked the rule does not apply and imaging is a clinical decision.

GCS < 15 / not alert, or hemodynamically unstable
Age < 16 years
Known vertebral disease (ankylosing spondylitis, RA, stenosis, prior cervical surgery)
Non-trauma presentation, or injury > 48 h ago

Step 1 — high-risk factors. Any present → imaging.

Age ≥ 65 years
Dangerous mechanism (fall > 1 m / 5 stairs, axial load, MVC > 100 km/h or rollover/ejection, motorised recreational vehicle, bicycle collision)
Paresthesias in extremities

Step 2 — low-risk factors. At least one required before rotation can be tested.

Simple rear-end MVC (excludes pushed into traffic, hit by bus/large truck, rollover, hit by high-speed vehicle)
Ambulatory at any time since injury
Sitting upright in the ED
Delayed onset of neck pain (not immediate)
Absence of midline cervical spine tenderness
Able to rotate 45° both ways
Unable to rotate 45°
Not yet assessed
Work through steps 0 → 3
The rule is applied in order — any high-risk factor means imaging regardless of the later steps.

Derived sensitivity 100%, specificity ~43% for clinically important cervical spine injury.
Source: Stiell et al., JAMA 2001.

😵 Canadian Syncope Risk Score — CSRS, −3 to +11

For adults presenting within 24 h of syncope after ED evaluation found no serious underlying cause. Not for persistent altered mental status, witnessed seizure, head trauma causing LOC, or severe intoxication.

Predisposition to vasovagal symptoms (warm crowded place, prolonged standing, fear, emotion, pain)−1

Select an ED diagnosis impression
CSRS cannot be scored until a diagnosis impression is chosen.
📊 CSRS Interpretation

Risk of a serious adverse event within 30 days of ED disposition — death, arrhythmia, MI, structural heart disease, PE, serious hemorrhage, or procedural intervention:

−3 to −2 — Very low
~0.3%. Discharge generally appropriate.


−1 to 0 — Low
~0.7%. Discharge with outpatient follow-up.


1 to 3 — Medium
Cautious disposition and close follow-up. Consider ~6 h of ED cardiac monitoring, and 15-day outpatient rhythm monitoring if discharged.


4 to 5 — High
Monitored admission and urgent cardiac evaluation.


6 to 11 — Very high
~51.3%. Admission on an urgent monitored pathway.


Percentages are the observed 30-day rates from the multicentre ED validation, for the very low, low and very high bands only. The medium and high bands carry guidance without a figure: that cohort does not report a single verified combined rate for them, and the numbers often quoted (~8% and ~19%) come from the earlier derivation cohort.

Monitoring: arrhythmias were detected within ~2 h in low-risk patients and within ~6 h in medium- and high-risk patients.

⚠️ CSRS does not apply when the ED workup has already identified a serious cause, and does not override clinical judgment.

Sources: derivation — Thiruganasambandamoorthy et al., CMAJ 2016 (PMID 27378464); validation — multicentre ED validation 2020 (PMID 32202605); monitoring — Circulation 2019 (PMID 30661373).

💊 COWS — Clinical Opiate Withdrawal Scale, 0–48

Score observed signs of opioid withdrawal. Where the patient had pain before withdrawal, score only the additional component attributable to withdrawal.

—
Score all 11 items
COWS guides timing of buprenorphine induction and symptom management.

Severity: 5–12 mild · 13–24 moderate · 25–36 moderately severe · >36 severe. Top-band labels vary between published versions of the form.

Local protocol: standard observed buprenorphine induction requires objective withdrawal at COWS ≥ 8; borderline COWS 8–9 favours start-low-go-slow. See ED-initiated buprenorphine →

Source: Wesson & Ling, J Psychoactive Drugs 2003;35(2):253–259.

🍺 CIWA-Ar — alcohol withdrawal, 0–67

Ten items — nine scored 0–7 plus orientation scored 0–4. Requires a cooperative, communicative patient; it is not a diagnostic test for withdrawal and performs poorly in delirium or when communication is impaired.

—
Score all 10 items
CIWA-Ar drives symptom-triggered benzodiazepine or phenobarbital dosing.

Bands used here: <8 minimal · 8–15 moderate · >15 severe.

⚠️ Published cutoffs genuinely differ — ≤10/11–15/≥16, <8/8–15/≥15, and 0–9/10–20/≥20 all appear in the literature. Your local symptom-triggered protocol governs the treatment threshold.
